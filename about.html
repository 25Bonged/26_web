The Mission Behind Vehicle Lab

Founded in 2023, Vehicle Lab was born out of a simple realization: the automotive engineering software landscape was stagnant. While vehicles were becoming smarter and more complex, the tools used to calibrate and diagnose them remained rooted in legacy workflows.

Our Engineering DNA

Our team consists of senior engineering architects who have worked at the intersection of automotive hardware and software. We don't just build software; we build precision instruments — from model-based calibration and AI automotive diagnostics to MDF4 signal analysis.

Commitment to Standards

We believe in open standards. That's why every tool we build — HELIX for calibration data management, XHandbook for EHANDBOOK-compatible ECU documentation — is natively compliant with ASAM, ISO (UDS/14229), and AUTOSAR specifications.

With over a decade of experience in automotive architecture and AI integration, Chayan leads the engineering vision at Vehicle Lab.
