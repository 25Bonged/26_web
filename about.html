The Mission Behind Vehicle Lab

Founded in 2024, Vehicle Lab was born out of a simple realization: the automotive engineering software landscape was stagnant. While vehicles were becoming smarter and more complex, the tools used to calibrate and diagnose them remained rooted in legacy workflows.

Engineering DNA

Vehicle Lab is built from the bench up by an engineer who has lived inside powertrain calibration, ECU diagnostics, and fleet data. We don't just write software; we build precision instruments — from model-based calibration and AI automotive diagnostics to MDF4 signal analysis.

Commitment to Standards

We believe in open standards. That's why every tool we build — HELIX for calibration data management, XHandbook for EHANDBOOK-compatible ECU documentation — is natively compliant with ASAM, ISO (UDS/14229), and AUTOSAR specifications.

Founder & Lead Engineer

Chayan Das

I'm an automotive software engineer who spends his days on the calibration and diagnostics bench — and his nights building the tools that should have existed there all along. Powertrain calibration at FEV, ECU diagnostics across the Stellantis and Bosch ecosystems, fleet and dyno data analysis at scale.

Everywhere I worked, the same pattern repeated: brilliant engineering, throttled by manual, legacy tooling. So I started building software to take the repetitive, high-skill grunt work off the engineer's plate — to reduce human work, not replace human judgment.

That turned into Vehicle Lab: a full calibration ecosystem in HELIX (A2L/HEX/DCM management, version control, AI anomaly detection), AI-driven diagnostics in DiagAI (multi-LLM agents, misfire-detection IP, WLTP/RDE/IUPR compliance automation), and a growing SaaS suite for automotive R&D teams and OEMs.
